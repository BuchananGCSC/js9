Image Processor

A classroom interface in the style of JS9-4L: large brightness, contrast and bias controls with a live pixel readout, paired with real menus. Students find the operations themselves — image math, blending, regions and colormaps all live in the menu bar.

  • Low/High brightness limits, stretch and bias
  • Live pixel value and position readout
  • Trimmed menus — enough to explore, not enough to drown
